Q: Saylani Welfare kya hai?
A: Saylani Welfare International Trust ek ghair-munafa organization hai jo khana, taleem, ilaj aur IT courses muft faraham karti hai.

Q: SMIT ka matalab kya hai?
A: SMIT ka matlab Saylani Mass IT Training hai.

Q: Main admission ke liye kaise apply karoon?
A: Admissions ke liye smit.saylaniwelfare.com par online form fill karein ya qareebi Saylani campus visit karein.

Q: Kya courses ki koi fee hai?
A: Nahi, Saylani ke tamam IT aur vocational courses 100% free hain.

Q: Saylani ka head office kahan hai?
A: Saylani Welfare ka head office Bahadurabad, Karachi mein hai.
